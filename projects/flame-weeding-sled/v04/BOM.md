## Tooling & Fabrication Consumables

- **Angle Grinder & Consumables**: $4.5''$ Angle Grinder, $1/16''$ Cut-Off Wheels, $36$-grit Grinding Flap Discs.
- **Welding Equipment**: Flux-Core MIG Welder (120V / 140A class), $.030''$ or $.035''$ E71T-11 Gasless Wire.
- **Drill & Bits**: Drill press or hand drill, $3/8''$ and $7/16''$ HSS twist drill bits.
- **Measuring Tools**: Soapstone marker, tape measure, steel square, angle finder / protractor.
