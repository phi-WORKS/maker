# DIY Fabrication & Assembly Guide — Towable Flame Weeding Sled (v1.2)

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for fabricating the **Towable Flame Weeding Sled** equipped with an overhead mounting frame for the **Harbor Freight Propane Torch with Push Button Igniter (Item #91037)**.

---

## 1. Safety & Workspace Preparation

> [!WARNING]
> **Safety Gear Required**: Welding helmet (Shade 10-11), leather welding gloves, heavy cotton/leather welding jacket, eye protection, ear protection, and respirator/dust mask when grinding steel.
>
> **Fire Safety**: Keep a Class ABC fire extinguisher or water hose nearby during welding and initial thermal testing.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Assembly Procedure

### Phase 1: Hood & Skirt Fabrication

1. **Cut Panels**:
   - Layout and cut 4 matching trapezoidal panels ($18''$ base, $4''$ top apex, $9.6''$ slant, $2''$ skirt) from 14-gauge mild steel sheet using an angle grinder cut-off wheel.
2. **Cut Rear Exhaust Vent**:
   - Cut out a $1.5'' \times 12.0''$ horizontal slot along the top of the rear skirt panel.
3. **Stitch-Weld Pyramid Hood**:
   - Tack weld 4 panels together into a pyramid hood shell. Verify $18'' \times 18''$ square base alignment.
   - Stitch weld external hip seams (1-inch welds staggered).

---

### Phase 2: Overhead Torch Mounting Frame

1. **Fabricate Overhead Bridge**:
   - Cut two $4.0''$ vertical legs and one $6.0''$ horizontal bridge from $1.5'' \times 3/16''$ flat bar stock.
   - Weld legs vertically to the outer top edges of the $4'' \times 4''$ apex plate.
2. **Attach Clamp Sleeve**:
   - Weld a $1.5''$ ID steel sleeve / ring to the bridge center, angled $35^\circ$ rearward (flame points toward the rear vent).
   - Insert the shaft of the Harbor Freight #91037 torch wand into the sleeve and tighten the clamping set screw.

---

### Phase 3: Skids, Corner Gussets & Front Hitch

1. **Mount Skid Runners**:
   - Form $30^\circ$ turned-up tips ($2.0''$ rise) on both ends of the two $22.0''$ flat bar skids.
   - Stitch weld runners to the lower edges of the left and right skirts.
2. **Weld Internal Corner Gussets**:
   - Fit 4 pieces of $1'' \times 1'' \times 1/8''$ angle iron ($6.0''$ long) internally across the lower base corners.
3. **Assemble Front Hitch & Forward Tow Bar**:
   - Weld double-ear clevis bracket ($3/16''$ plate) to the FRONT skirt center.
   - Weld $20^\circ$ angle stop tab under bracket.
   - Connect $5.0\text{ ft}$ square tow bar tube extending FORWARD from front clevis pin.

---

## 3. Operational Safety Checklist

- [ ] **Flame Direction Check**: Verify torch nozzle points rearward (away from operator pulling forward at the front).
- [ ] **Torch Rigidity**: Confirm Harbor Freight 91037 torch wand is clamped securely into the overhead frame.
- [ ] **Hose Connection**: Leak test high-pressure hose fittings with soapy water before igniting.
- [ ] **Forward Towing**: Always walk forward pulling the T-handle grip ahead of the sled.
