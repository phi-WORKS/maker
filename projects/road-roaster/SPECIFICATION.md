# Road Roaster — Master Technical Specification

> **Towable Thermal Weed Shock Sled**  
> *Single-Source Architectural & Engineering Specification*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Design Purpose

The **Road Roaster** is a towable thermal weed shock sled designed for agricultural, gravel driveway, and roadside non-chemical thermal weed management.

### Core Design Objectives
1. **Thermal Efficiency**: Closed $18'' \times 18''$ pyramidal shroud traps heat generated by the propane torch wand, increasing ground-level thermal transfer to plant cell walls.
2. **Onboard Fuel Storage**: Integrated quick-release bottle harness mounted directly onto the upper tow bar tube accepts standard 1 lb propane cylinders.
3. **Ergonomic Tow Rigging**: 5 ft rigid square tube tow bar with front clevis hitch and $20^\circ$ drop-stop rest tab allows walking or ATV towing.
4. **Modularity**: Consumes external commercial component CAD assets (`torch_hf91037`, `propane_cylinder_1lb`, `propane_harness`) imported directly from `components/`.

## 4. Materials & Manufacturing Specifications

- **Hood & Skirts**: 14-gauge Hot-Rolled Mild Steel Sheet.
- **Skid Runners**: $1.5'' \times 3/16''$ Steel Flat Bar.
- **Tow Bar & Frame**: $3/4'' \times 3/4'' \times 1/8''$ Steel Square Tubing.
- **Torch Component**: Harbor Freight #91037 ($500,000\text{ BTU/hr}$ Propane Torch with Piezo Igniter).
- **Fuel Canister**: Standard 1 lb Disposable/Refillable Propane Cylinder.
