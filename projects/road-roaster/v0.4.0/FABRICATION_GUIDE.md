# Fabrication & Assembly Guide — Road Roaster Version 1.4.0

Step-by-step instructions for fabricating and assembling the **Road Roaster Towable Flame Weeding Sled (v1.4.0)** equipped with an onboard **1 lb Propane Bottle Harness** and **Harbor Freight #91037 Torch Mount**.

---

## Step 1: Hood Shell & Skid Runner Fabrication

1. **Cut Sheet Panels**: Cut four 14-gauge mild steel trapezoid panels ($18''$ base, $4''$ top, $6''$ rise) and four $18'' \times 2''$ skirt panels according to [CUT_LIST.md](CUT_LIST.md).
2. **Pyramid Hood Assembly**: Tack-weld vertical skirt panels to sloped trapezoid panels. Weld the four corner seams inside and outside to form the sealed $18'' \times 18'' \times 8''$ total canopy shell.
3. **Rear Heat Vent**: Cut the $12'' \times 1.5''$ notch centered into the rear skirt panel to release excess heat plume rearward away from tow operator.
4. **Skid Runners**: Bend front and rear $2''$ tips of the dual $1.5'' \times 3/16''$ flat bar runners up 30°. Weld runners under the perimeter flange flush with outer sides.
5. **Corner Gussets**: Weld the four triangular angle iron gussets across the bottom base corners for rigid anti-racking structural support.

---

## Step 2: Overhead Torch Support Frame

1. **Frame Fabrication**: Weld two $80\text{ mm}$ vertical legs and one $152.4\text{ mm}$ horizontal bridge from $1.5'' \times 3/16''$ flat bar over the apex opening.
2. **Wand Sleeve**: Weld the $60\text{ mm}$ wand clamping sleeve centered on the cross bridge at a $35^\circ$ forward angle pointing toward the pull operator.

---

## Step 3: Tow Bar Rigging & Clevis Stop Tab

1. **Front Clevis Hitch**: Weld dual $2'' \times 2'' \times 3/16''$ clevis ears onto front flange centered with $1.0''$ inner spacing.
2. **Drop-Stop Rest Tab**: Weld the $1.5'' \times 3/16''$ stop tab under clevis ears at a $20^\circ$ downward angle to prevent the tow bar from dropping below $20^\circ$ when unhitched.
3. **Tow Bar & T-Grip**: Drill a $3/8''$ pin hole $0.75''$ from front end of $5\text{ ft}$ square tube. Weld $8''$ round tube T-grip to top end.

---

## Step 4: Onboard Propane Harness & Bottle Mounting

1. **Harness Fabrication / Assembly**: Assemble or mount the `components/propane_harness/` bottle cage (bottom seat cup, side retention arms, quick-latch strap, and rear mounting spine plate).
2. **Tow Bar Attachment**: Position the harness spine plate onto the upper section of the tow bar ($43.3'' / 1100\text{ mm}$ from lower clevis pin). Secure using dual $3/4''$ square tube saddle clamps.
3. **Bottle Seating**: Insert a standard 1 lb disposable or refillable propane cylinder into the harness seat cup. Tighten the red knurled quick-release thumb-screw strap around the bottle body.
4. **Gas Line Connection**: Thread a flexible high-pressure propane extension hose onto the 1"-20 UNEF cylinder top valve. Route hose along tow bar tube and connect fitting to brass valve knob on torch handle.

---

## Step 5: Torch Installation & Safety Pre-Check

1. **Torch Mounting**: Slide Harbor Freight #91037 chrome wand through overhead sleeve ring. Recess nozzle bell $1.5''$ into apex chamber. Tighten sleeve set screw.
2. **Leak Inspection**: Apply soapy water solution around cylinder valve connection and hose fittings; check for bubble formation.
3. **Ignition Test**: Open cylinder valve 1/2 turn, open handle brass knob slightly, and push red piezo igniter button to verify smooth burner ignition.
